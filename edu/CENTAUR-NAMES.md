# Naming the centaur — the mythological roster

> **DECISION (the author, 2026-07-31): the robot is named _Alpha Centauri_** — the
> alpha build of the Centauri line, and the nearest star system to home
> (which lives inside the constellation Centaurus, usually identified with
> Chiron). Roster below kept for future builds/sub-assemblies.

Context: the TelescopeBot pivot to a quadruped-humanoid hybrid (telescoping
torso on a four-legged base). Greek myth has two distinct centaur lineages,
and the split is useful:

- **The wild tribe** — descended from Ixion and Nephele (the cloud Zeus
  shaped as a decoy Hera). Their son Centaurus sired the race on the
  Magnesian mares of Mount Pelion. Brawlers, wine-mad, the reason
  "centauromachy" is a word.
- **The civilized exceptions** — Chiron (son of Cronus in stallion form and
  the Oceanid Philyra) and Pholus (son of Silenus and an ash-nymph).
  Different parentage, different nature: learned, hospitable, kind. Both
  died by Heracles' hydra-poisoned arrows, both by accident.

A robot is presumably from the second lineage.

## The candidates

| Name | Who they were | Personality fit | Namespace notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chiron** | The wisest being in Greek myth. Tutor of Achilles, Jason, Asclepius; master of medicine, music, archery, prophecy. Immortal, but agonizingly wounded by a stray poisoned arrow; traded his immortality to free Prometheus and was set in the stars (usually the constellation Centaurus). | The teacher-healer. Fits a robot built inside an `edu/` project that exists to learn. | Heavily used: Bugatti Chiron, medical brands, asteroid 2060 Chiron — which made "centaur" the official name for its whole asteroid class. |
| **Asbolus** | "Sooty" / "charcoal-black." The centaurs' **seer** — an augur who read omens in the flight of birds. Warned the tribe not to fight Heracles; they ignored him. | The one who *sees what's coming*. A look→reason→act vision robot named for a prophet who reads moving shapes in the air is almost on the nose. Bonus: "sooty" suits a dark-PETG print. | Very clean namespace (one asteroid, 8405 Asbolus). Unique, greppable, pronounceable. |
| **Pholus** | The hospitable custodian of the centaurs' communal wine. Hosted Heracles kindly; the opened wine jar maddened the wild tribe into the famous brawl. Died *examining* a poisoned arrow — curious how something so small could kill — and dropped it on his own hoof. | The curious, friendly host — killed by unsafe handling of a dangerous artifact. Given how much of this repo is safety guards, tiers, and e-stops, Pholus is the cautionary patron saint. | Clean (asteroid 5145 Pholus). |
| **Centaurus** | The progenitor — son of Ixion and the cloud, father of the whole race. Also the southern constellation holding **Alpha Centauri**, the nearest star system. | "The first of its kind." Apt for a Mark 1 that may sire a product line. | Shared with the constellation/star; slightly generic. |
| **Hylonome** | The only named female centaur (Ovid). Groomed, gentle, civilized; devoted to Cyllarus — when he fell at the wedding brawl she chose to die on the same spear. | Grace and devotion; the gentle counterpoint to the brawlers. | Clean, uncommon, pretty. |
| **Cyllarus** | The beautiful golden young centaur, beloved of Hylonome, killed in the Centauromachy. | Elegance, youth. | Also the name of Castor's horse in some sources. |
| **Nessus** | The ferryman who assaulted Deianira mid-river; shot by Heracles; engineered posthumous revenge — his tainted blood became the "Shirt of Nessus" that eventually killed Heracles. | Cunning and patient; a great villain name if you want the robot to have menace. | Collides hard with Tenable Nessus, the vulnerability scanner every security person knows. |
| **Eurytion** | The drunk whose grab at the bride Hippodamia ignited the Centauromachy. | The cautionary tale about operating under impaired control loops. | Clean but inglorious. |
| **Rhoetus** | Among the fiercest brawlers of Ovid's Centauromachy. | Aggression. | Obscure. |
| **Elatus** | The centaur whose arm the fateful arrow passed *through* before lodging in Chiron's knee. | A footnote in someone else's tragedy. | Obscure. |
| **Crotus** | Strictly a satyr-archer (son of Pan), raised by the Muses, inventor of archery and applause — but he is the usual identification of **Sagittarius**, so he's drawn as a centaur everywhere. | The archer; rhythm and precision. | Clean. |

Minor Ovidian names, if a sub-assembly ever needs one: Petraeus ("of the
rocks"), Thaumas ("wonder"), Bienor, Amycus, Medon.

Beyond Greece: **Polkan** (Russian lubok hero via the Italian Pulicane —
half-man half-hound/horse, superhuman speed and loyalty), **Kinnara**
(Indic celestial musicians, part human part horse or bird), the
**Onocentaur** (donkey-centaur of the bestiaries), and the medieval
**Ipotanes**.

## Shortlist, mapped to this machine

1. **Asbolus** — the seer, for a robot whose entire loop is perception →
   prediction → action; sooty-black like its filament; clean namespace.
2. **Pholus** — the kind, curious host; patron of handling dangerous things
   carefully (this repo's whole safety culture).
3. **Chiron** — the teacher, if iconic beats unique.
4. **Centaurus** — the progenitor, if this is Mark 1 of a line.
5. **Hylonome** — the graceful one.
